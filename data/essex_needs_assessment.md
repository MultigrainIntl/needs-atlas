# Essex County Needs Assessment — August 2026

_Demographic figures are live Census values; health/ALICE/provider indicators pending (listed below)._

## Poverty, income, and the case for concentrated need
The communities Zufall Health serves within Essex County, New Jersey include urbanized areas with pockets of concentrated poverty and limited access to care. Essex County's median household income is $80,789, below the statewide median of $103,556, and 13.4% of residents live in poverty, above the New Jersey rate of 9.2%.[1] Beneath the county average, need concentrates sharply: 106 of 211 census tracts exceed the statewide uninsured rate, and the highest-need tract shows 0.0% uninsured with 100.0% of residents below 200% of the federal poverty level.[2]

## Barriers to care: insurance and providers
Access to coverage is a clear barrier. 13.2% of Essex County residents under age 65 are uninsured, above the statewide rate of 9.1%.[1] Provider-supply measures — primary-care and mental-health provider-to-population ratios — are pending (see below) and are expected to reinforce this access gap.

## Demographics and language access
Essex County is home to 896,379 residents. 27.4% identify as Hispanic or Latino and 40.5% as Black or African American, compared with 24.0% and 15.8% statewide.[1] Language access is a substantial need: 37.8% of residents age 5 and over speak a language other than English at home, above the New Jersey figure of 33.1%.[1] Adults age 65 and over make up 14.5% of the population (New Jersey: 18.5%).[1]
