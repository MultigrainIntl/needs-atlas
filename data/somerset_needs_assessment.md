# Somerset County Needs Assessment — August 2026

_County/state figures are published Census values; the tract concentration analysis is from ACS 5-year tract tables. ALICE / provider / chronic-disease / food-insecurity indicators pending (listed below) — not estimated._

## Poverty, income, and the case for concentrated need
The communities Zufall Health serves within Somerset County, New Jersey include areas of concentrated poverty and constrained access to care. Somerset County's median household income is $140,374, above the statewide median of $103,556, and 5.8% of residents live in poverty, below the New Jersey rate of 9.2%.[1] County averages, however, flatten the neighborhoods where need concentrates — which a census-tract view brings into focus.

## Where the need concentrates: a census-tract view
Somerset County contains 74 census tracts. Measured against the seven-county service-area average, 19 of those tracts carry an above-average uninsured rate and 13 carry an above-average share of residents below 200% of the federal poverty level.[2] 12% of the county's residents — roughly 42,329 people — live in tracts where at least one in four residents falls below 200% of the federal poverty level. The sharpest need sits in and around Census Tract 517 (GEOID 34035051700): there, 27% of residents are uninsured and 48% live below 200% of the federal poverty level, and 18% of households are limited-English-speaking — a concentration that county-level averages hide entirely. Across the seven counties Zufall serves, Somerset carries the fourth-highest composite need. This is precisely the sub-county geography a grant application must document, and it is where Zufall's sites and outreach can be targeted for greatest effect.

## Barriers to care: insurance and providers
Access to coverage is a clear barrier. 6.4% of Somerset County residents under age 65 are uninsured, below the statewide rate of 9.1%.[1] Provider-supply measures — primary-care, dental, and mental-health provider-to-population ratios — are pending source connection (see below) and are expected to reinforce this access gap.

## Demographics and language access
Somerset County is home to 356,486 residents. 18.9% identify as Hispanic or Latino and 11.3% as Black or African American, compared with 24.0% and 15.8% statewide.[1] Language access is a substantial need: 34.7% of residents age 5 and over speak a language other than English at home, above the New Jersey figure of 33.1%.[1] Adults age 65 and over make up 18.9% of the population (New Jersey: 18.5%).[1]
