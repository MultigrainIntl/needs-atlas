# Somerset County Needs Assessment — August 2026

_Demographic figures are live Census values; health/ALICE/provider indicators pending (listed below)._

## Poverty, income, and the case for concentrated need
The communities Zufall Health serves within Somerset County, New Jersey include urbanized areas with pockets of concentrated poverty and limited access to care. Somerset County's median household income is $140,374, above the statewide median of $103,556, and 5.8% of residents live in poverty, below the New Jersey rate of 9.2%.[1] County-wide averages, however, understate need that concentrates in specific urban communities; tract-level analysis (in progress) is required to locate it precisely.

## Barriers to care: insurance and providers
Access to coverage is a clear barrier. 6.4% of Somerset County residents under age 65 are uninsured, below the statewide rate of 9.1%.[1] Provider-supply measures — primary-care and mental-health provider-to-population ratios — are pending (see below) and are expected to reinforce this access gap.

## Demographics and language access
Somerset County is home to 356,486 residents. 18.9% identify as Hispanic or Latino and 11.3% as Black or African American, compared with 24.0% and 15.8% statewide.[1] Language access is a substantial need: 34.7% of residents age 5 and over speak a language other than English at home, above the New Jersey figure of 33.1%.[1] Adults age 65 and over make up 18.9% of the population (New Jersey: 18.5%).[1]
